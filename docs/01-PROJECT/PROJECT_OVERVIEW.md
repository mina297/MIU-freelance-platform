# MIU Freelance Platform — Project Overview

| Field | Value |

## 2. Project Vision

To create a trusted university-focused marketplace where students can turn academic skills into practical freelance experience and connect with real clients.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between university learning and professional work.

## 3. Problem Statement

Students may have valuable technical, creative, and professional skills but limited opportunities to demonstrate them to clients.

Clients may also find it difficult to discover suitable student talent through a dedicated university-oriented platform.

The proposed system addresses this gap by combining student profiles, verified participation, projects, proposals, services, and order management.

## 4. Proposed Solution

MIU Freelance Platform integrates two approaches.

### Project-Based Marketplace

Clients create projects with requirements, budgets, and deadlines.

Students submit proposals specifying:
- Proposed price in EGP
- Delivery time
- Cover message
- Relevant attachments

Clients can compare proposals and select a suitable freelancer.

### Service-Based Marketplace

Students create predefined service listings.

A service may include:
- Description
- Category and skills
- Packages
- Optional extras
- EGP price
- Delivery time
- Portfolio examples

Clients select a service and place an order.

## 5. Target Users

- Visitors
- MIU student freelancers
- Clients, including external clients
- Super Admin
- Verification Committee
- Marketplace Operations Committee
- Finance Committee
- Support and Disputes Committee
- AI and Quality Committee

## 6. Core Functional Areas

### Identity and Access
Account registration, login, password reset, role permissions, and planned identity verification.

### Marketplace
Project publishing, proposals, service listings, search, filtering, and recommendations.

### Collaboration
Order workspaces, deadlines, messages, attachments, submissions, and revisions.

### Financial Workflows
EGP checkout, sandbox escrow, transaction records, refunds, payouts, and administrative financial review.

### Reputation
Student profiles, portfolios, ratings, and reviews.

### AI Assistance
Planned identity assistance, matching, chatbot, translation, moderation, and quality monitoring.

### Governance
Super Admin, five specialized committees, approval workflows, escalations, and audit trails.

## 7. Key Differentiators

- University-focused freelancer discovery
- Both projects/proposals and service packages
- Planned MIU identity verification
- Dedicated administrative committees
- AI-assisted discovery and quality monitoring
- One-currency EGP marketplace
- Unified profiles, orders, support, and reputation

## 8. Development Approach

### Phase 1
Requirements, interface prototypes, responsive frontend, and simulated user journeys.

### Phase 2
Backend, persistent data, authentication, and approved external integrations.

### Phase 3
Mobile packaging, complete testing, and final system delivery.

> The three-phase technical roadmap is a team development plan. The academic Phase 1 submission requirements are defined separately in the instructor's announcement.

## 9. Expected Impact

The proposed platform may help:
- Students develop professional portfolios.
- Students gain experience delivering client work.
- Clients discover student talent.
- The university explore structured student freelancing opportunities.
- Future student teams extend a documented software platform.

These are project objectives, not measured outcomes.

## 10. Important Constraints

- EGP is the only currency.
- Committee members cannot self-register.
- Identity and financial data require appropriate protection.
- AI-assisted decisions need human review paths.
- Live money movement requires approval and provider integration.
- Capacitor mobile packaging is not part of Phase 1.

## 11. Success Criteria

Success will be evaluated through:
- Coverage of approved requirements
- Complete and understandable user journeys
- Usable responsive interfaces
- Correct role-specific behavior
- Reliable simulated marketplace scenarios
- Quality documentation
- Maintainable technical structure
